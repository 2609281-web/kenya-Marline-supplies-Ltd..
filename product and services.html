Who We Are

Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd is a Kenyan company established to serve the needs of customers in the marine industry. The company supplies boats, marine equipment and transport services to fishermen, boat operators, tourism businesses and shipping companies.

Our Mission and Vision

Our mission is to provide reliable marine products and quality transport services to our customers.

Our vision is to become a trusted provider of marine supplies and transport solutions in Kenya.

Our Operations in Kenya

We serve customers along the Kenyan coastline and around Lake Victoria. Our activities include boat sales, passenger and cargo transport, boat hire and the supply of marine safety and navigation equipment.
