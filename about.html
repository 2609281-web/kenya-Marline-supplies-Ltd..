Who We Are

Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd is a Kenyan company established to serve the needs of customers in the marine. The company supplies boats, marine equipment and transport services to fishermen, and shipping companies.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide marine products and quality transport services to our customers.

Our vision is to become a trusted provider of marine supplies in Kenya.

Our Operations in Kenya

We serve customers along the Kenyan coastline . Our activities include boat sales, boat hire and the supply of marine safety and navigation equipment.
